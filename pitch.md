# LoreLich Vault — Pitch

## The Problem

Ancestral stories are dying.

Oral histories, family narratives, cultural wisdom — they live in the memories of elders and are lost when those elders pass. Digital files rot, cloud services shut down, hard drives fail. Institutions archive for institutions, not for families or communities.

There is no permanent, community-owned, AI-enhanced home for the stories that define who we are.

And when those stories do survive — documentarians, filmmakers, and researchers have no structured way to discover, license, or build on them. The value stays locked. Families see none of the returns.

## The Solution

**LoreLich Vault** is a sacred digital archive where ancestral stories live forever — and now earn.

- **Permanent storage** via 0G decentralized network — no single company can delete your grandmother's voice
- **Cryptographic ownership** via soulbound NFTs — your story, provably yours, non-transferable
- **Privacy by default** — client-side AES-256 encryption means only you and who you choose can hear private vaults
- **AI guardian** — LoreLich, a Groq-powered cultural AI, helps you query, explore, and remix stories with reverence
- **Verifiable provenance** — DA proofs mean every story upload is verifiably on-chain, forever
- **IP Licensing Layer** — story owners set programmable on-chain license terms; filmmakers and developers request licenses; royalties auto-flow via pull-pattern withdrawals
- **Documentary Pitch Portal** — LoreLich AI auto-generates film treatment briefs (logline, synopsis, visual approach, comparables, target audience) for any licensable story; filmmakers can browse and request directly
- **Genealogy Tree Builder** — import GEDCOM family tree files, visualize ancestors as an interactive constellation graph, and let LoreLich AI suggest which vault stories belong to which ancestor

## How It Works

```
Upload Story → Set License Terms → LoreLich Generates Film Brief
                                         ↓
Filmmaker Browses /marketplace → Reads /pitch/[storyId] → Requests License
                                         ↓
Owner Approves → Royalty Flows On-Chain → Owner Withdraws Anytime
```

Meanwhile, families build their constellation at `/tree`:
- Import GEDCOM from Ancestry, MyHeritage, or FamilySearch
- Visualize ancestors as a star map
- Link stories to ancestors — manually or via AI suggestion
- Save the entire tree to 0G decentralized storage

## The Market

- 8 billion people have ancestors
- 800M+ diaspora people actively seeking cultural connection
- $4.5B genealogy market (growing 13% YoY)
- $12B+ documentary and factual entertainment market
- 300M+ podcast listeners would engage with family audio archives
- IP licensing market for cultural content is structurally underserved — no on-chain primitive exists today

## The Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Platform license fee | 2.5% of every on-chain royalty payment (built into `LoreIPModule.sol`) |
| Premium storage tiers | Larger vault quotas, priority 0G pinning |
| Pitch Portal featured listings | Promoted placement for licensable stories |
| Enterprise genealogy vaults | Institutional and diaspora community plans |

The platform fee is code, not policy — it is immutable, transparent, and collected without human intervention.

## Why Now

Three things converged:

1. **0G Network** launched Galileo Testnet — permanent, verifiable decentralized storage at cost
2. **Groq free tier** makes AI querying and treatment generation viable at zero marginal cost
3. **ERC5192 soulbound standard** matures — cryptographic ownership without speculation

LoreLich Vault is the first product to combine all three into a complete cultural IP stack.
