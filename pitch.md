# LoreLich Vault — Pitch

## The Problem

Ancestral stories are dying.

Oral histories, family narratives, cultural wisdom — they live in the memories of elders and are lost when those elders pass. Digital files rot, cloud services shut down, hard drives fail. Institutions archive for institutions, not for families or communities.

There is no permanent, community-owned, AI-enhanced home for the stories that define who we are.

## The Solution

**LoreLich Vault** is a sacred digital archive where ancestral stories live forever.

- **Permanent storage** via 0G decentralized network — no single company can delete your grandmother's voice
- **Cryptographic ownership** via soulbound NFTs — your story, provably yours, non-transferable
- **Privacy by default** — client-side AES-256 encryption means only you and who you choose can hear private vaults
- **AI guardian** — LoreLich, a Claude-powered cultural AI, helps you query, explore, and remix stories with reverence
- **Verifiable provenance** — DA proofs mean every story upload is verifiably on-chain, forever

## The Market

- 8 billion people have ancestors
- 800M+ diaspora people actively seeking cultural connection
- $4.5B genealogy market (growing 13% YoY)
- 300M+ podcast listeners would engage with family audio archives

## The Vision

By 2030: Every family has a digital vault. Every story that matters is preserved.

LoreLich is the infrastructure layer for cultural memory.
